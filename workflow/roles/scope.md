# Scope role

Read the roadmap stage and every dependency named there. Produce or update the open closeout record with objectives, in-scope work, non-goals, dependencies, performance target, security/offline target, known limitations, unresolved risks, and human stop conditions.

Do not implement code. Do not broaden the stage into later MVPs. Finish only when the closeout record contains concrete, testable boundaries and the workflow check passes for an open stage.
