# Scenario Intervention Example

At 08:20 the scenario engine emits an authorized `CloseInfrastructure(bridge-B)` command/event.

It does not issue `Panic()` to nearby residents.

Consequences emerge through:

- route invalidation;
- congestion;
- direct perception;
- messages/posts/news;
- schedule disruption;
- organizational response;
- resource/service effects.
