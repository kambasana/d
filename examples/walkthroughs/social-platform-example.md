# Virtual Social Platform Example

A platform adapter exposes typed actions such as CreatePost, ViewPost, LikePost, Repost, Comment, Follow, Mute, Block, DirectMessage, and Report.

Feed ranking selects exposure based on declared platform rules such as follow graph, recency, eligibility, topic affinity, engagement, privacy, and moderation.

The simulation records separately:

- post exposure;
- claim extraction/understanding;
- belief update;
- visible social reaction;
- retransmission;
- inferred motive if any.
