# Building Occupancy and Semantic Zoom Example

At city zoom the hospital contributes to a geographic population cluster.

At street zoom:

```text
Central Hospital - 1,284 occupants
```

Selecting the building can reveal:

```text
Ground: 274
Floor 1: 301
Floor 2: 184
Floor 3: 219
Floor 4: 146
Basement: 160
```

Selecting a room with 9 co-located agents may use radial expansion for selection. Their expanded icons remain visually tethered to the room/point and their authoritative positions do not change.
